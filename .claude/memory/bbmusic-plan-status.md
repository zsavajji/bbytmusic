What the user decided on 2026-11-14:
- bbtube is not the base; DASH and SABR are wanted: "we can do better than the old BBtube youtube client… we need to take advantage of newer streams such as DASH and SABR".
- Engine in C++20 with libcurl + wolfSSL, not Go.
- Steps: Phases 0–4 as proposed; SponsorBlock and the equalizer in Phase 3; Phase 5 is translations and hardening only; lyrics, downloads, normalisation, crossfade, sleep timer and quality settings are "not for now".
- Opus decoded by us to libasound; no PO tokens to start; the Q10 on 10.3.3 is the reference; music must survive swiping the app away (so the engine is a headless process, and it holds the system's now-playing service itself over PPS).
- Unattended work: "split the code in 500-ish lines max per class", "commit every relevant step on the repo", "you may use 1 opus and 2 sonnet subagents for delegation, with the system rules", "marking relevant decisions to be made".
- The phone: "you may use the phone read write via SSH for your tests". I install test builds with `scripts/install-phone.sh` and push probes to `/root/bbmusic`; no reboots, no system settings.
- UI direction: "i would like this, and the player in particular to resemble the stock player with a Youtube flavor, but with the functions the UI has on Windows phone. i will provide screenshots". The mini-player is the title plus one Play/Pause icon; other actions go on the full player. The screenshots came on 2026-11-14 and the redesign is built (unseen).
- When they report several problems: "please check them all, and fix, build only once after fix".
- Their UI brief (2026-11-14): search with "clearer separation between albums and songs", "album art preview and informations about the tracks", an album view, the queue "with possibility to rearrange and sort", the player "inspire from the stock BB10 player, but with the moder functionality expressed in the WP interface", "the miniplayer can also be reduced to a strip with just the title", "trying to stay as Cascades as possible". **Why:** the user works across sessions and asked to "save everything for tomorrow".
**The UI cannot be checked without the user:** over root ssh I found no safe way to launch the app or grab the screen (`screencapture` is the system's service with no obvious control object; `snapshot -h` hangs, do not run it). So: lint (`cascades-qmllint.sh`; it does not check handler names in grouped properties nor JS method names), keep new tabs and pages loaded on demand (Tab `delegate`, `ComponentDefinition`) so only `main.qml`, `SearchPage`, `MiniPlayer`, `ConnectionBar` can stop the start, run the engine on the phone from a shell (`scripts/push-phone.sh`, `ssh -L`), and have a read-only sub-agent review the QML against the qmltypes and SDK headers (two rounds on 2026-11-14 found real faults).
